Trace dc Removal
Trace dc Removal removes dc bias or offset from the input
traces.

Theory
The two possible ways for the program to calculate the level of
the dc component in the trace are:
• The arithmetic mean of the samples.
• The mean of the minimum and maximum sample to
determine dc level.
Previously applied mutes are automatically reapplied after
the dc bias has been removed.

Parameters
Type of central value
Select the method to compute the dc level. The possible
choices are:
• Mean uses the mean as the central value. This is the
more usual approach, and should be zero on a trace with
no bias
• (Min+Max)/2 uses the mean of the minimum and maximum as the central value.
