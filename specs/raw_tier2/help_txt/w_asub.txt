SRME Adaptive Subtraction
Given an ensemble of Data traces and Noise traces (the Noise
traces output from SRME, for instance), this module
adaptively subtracts the Noise traces from the corresponding
Data traces.

Theory
This adaptation involves the design and application of
temporal ﬁlters to the noise such that the resulting estimate
of the signal has minumum energy. These ﬁlters can change
both temporally and spatially by appropriate selection of the
windowing parameters.
For some cases the noise estimated by state-of-the-art
algorithms fails to fully model the actual target noise ﬁeld in
the recorded data. Examples of this characteristic mismatch
are coherent noise estimated from suboptimally sampled 3D
data and multiple estimates produced by the SRME
nonlinear algorithm. In cases such as these, an improved
attenuation of the target noise ﬁeld may be achieved by using
the adaptive subtraction strategy contained in this module.
It is very important to use this approach with some caution.
Since the basic criterion of the ﬁlter design is the
minimization of the resultant energy in the ’signal’, an
infelicitious choice of parameters has the danger of attacking
the true reﬂection signal of interest. Hence, it is
recommended practice to test a suite of candidate windowing
parameters and choose one that preserves the signal as well
as reducing the noise.
In order to help ensure that the adaptive ﬁlter is matching
noise in the estimate to noise in the original data, it can be
helpful to design the ﬁlter by averaging the required
correlations spatially. This facility is provided with the
’spatially averging size’ parameter described below.
If the input data contains high amplitude spurious energy,
the adaptive ﬁltering algorithm may attempt to reduce this
energy by severely distorting the noise estimate. In order to
reduce the possibility of this signiﬁcantly distorting the signal

Usage
Input to this tool should be ensembles containing Data traces
and Noise traces for a speciﬁc ensemble. One example of the
input, for instance, would be the output from SRME Match
Filter.
Output is ensembles with Noise adaptively subtracted from
Data.

Parameters
Input data organization
Four options are available here:
• DDD...NNN-Panel of data + panel of noise
• DNDN...DN-Data and noise alternate in ensemble
• NNN...DDD Panel of noise + panel of data
• NDND...ND-Noise and data alternate in ensemble
Output option
Two options are available here:
• Signal-Output the estimated signal
• Noise-Output the ﬁltered noise
Temporal window length (ms)
Length of the temporal design window (ms). This should be at
least 5 times the chosen ﬁlter length.
Filter length (ms)
Length of the adaptive ﬁlter (ms). This should be less that
20% of the temporal window length.
Spatial averaging size
Radius of circle (in ft or m) over which to average in ﬁlter
design (0 means no spatial averaging).
